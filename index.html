    Know the site before you walk it

    Search a client or prospect to pull their OSHA history, injury rates, severe injury reports, EPA and MSHA records, and any trucks they run.

    Pick the right location

    OSHA records the same business under different names and addresses. Match on the address, not just the name.

    Find accounts that are getting hurt

    Wisconsin manufacturers and contractors whose injury rates run above their industry, from their own OSHA 300A filings. Bad experience means high comp costs and a reason to take your call.

    Regions are approximate, by ZIP prefix. “Over expected” is how many more injuries they logged than an average shop in their industry with the same hours. It favors bigger accounts on purpose.

    What hurts people in this industry

    Wisconsin severe injury reports and 300A filings rolled up by industry. No employer names, so it’s safe to put in front of a client.

    Walk the site

    Check items as you go. Snap photos and dictate notes on findings. Everything saves on this phone, so a dead signal won’t cost you the visit.
